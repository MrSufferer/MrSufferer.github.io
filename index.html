Payments & escrow

FleetPay

Avalanche Fuji MVP

Send a payment to an email address, before the recipient has a wallet.

An on-chain escrow lets recipients verify their email and claim to an Avalanche address. Pending funds can enter a capped yield vault; positive realized yield is shared on successful claims. Includes cancellation, expiry, and a fiat conversion preview.

Why this matters

Shows the full payment journey: onboarding, escrow state, recipient claims, and integration with external yield protocols.
